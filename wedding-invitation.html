Trips & Tips

✨ Discover Budapest

Budapest is truly a wonderful city — vibrant, historic, elegant, and full of character.

We recommend spending 3–4 days here, which is more than enough time to explore the highlights, enjoy amazing food, relax in a thermal bath, and soak in the atmosphere without rushing.

There are plenty of well-known historic sights to explore.

We recommend using the Visit a City app for a tailor-made sightseeing plan. It allows you to organize your trip based on the number of days you're staying and creates a structured daily itinerary around the city highlights.

🌟 Must-See Highlights

  • • The Hungarian Parliament – one of Europe's most beautiful parliament buildings
  • • Fisherman's Bastion – fairy-tale views over the Danube
  • • Buda Castle & the Castle District – charming cobblestone streets and historic atmosphere
  • • The Chain Bridge – especially magical at night
  • • St. Stephen's Basilica – climb the dome for panoramic views
  • • Heroes' Square & City Park, including the stunning Vajdahunyad Castle
  • • A sunset walk along the Danube Promenade
  • • A ruin bar experience in District VII for something uniquely Budapest

🌙 Beyond the Famous Attractions

  • • New York Café It's the perfect place for a coffee and cake break after sightseeing, or even for an elegant brunch.
  • • Danube Cruise A cruise on the Danube is one of the most beautiful ways to experience Budapest — whether with a glass of champagne or a full-course dinner cruise.
  • • Hop-On Hop-Off Bus It might sound commercial, but in Budapest it is genuinely worth it. There is so much history to discover, and the audio guide provides a structured and informative overview of the city.
  • • Gellért Hill & Liberty Statue Make sure that at least one evening you climb up Gellért Hill to the Liberty Statue for one of the most epic views of Budapest and the Danube. During the day, the panorama is breathtaking — but at night, when the city lights reflect on the river, Budapest truly elevates to another level.
  • • Budapest Labyrinth & Hospital in the Rock Two very special historical experiences: the mysterious underground Labyrinth beneath the Buda hills (stretching over 10 km), and the fascinating former emergency hospital and Cold War nuclear bunker.
  • • Sunrise Run Along the Danube If you enjoy running — or if you've partied until sunrise — we highly recommend going out early in the morning for a run along the Danube between the bridges. Climbing toward Gellért Hill near the Elisabeth Bridge (the white bridge) at sunrise is truly unforgettable.
  • • Hungarian Jewish Heritage & 20th Century History Visit the House of Terror museum (dedicated to the victims of the Nazi and Communist regimes), see the moving memorial Shoes on the Danube Bank, and explore the Dohány Street Synagogue — one of the largest in Europe and a central landmark of the historic Jewish Quarter.

🌮 Our Favourite Restaurants

  • • Paprika Vendéglő Very Hungarian and the goulash is epic!! If you visit Heroes' Square, it's just a short 10-minute walk away.
  • • VakVarjú A Hungarian restaurant with amazing food. Slightly more upscale than Paprika, but a bit less "cute" and traditional in atmosphere.
  • • Menza Étterem és Kávéház Stylish local favorite at Liszt Ferenc tér, blending Hungarian ingredients with a modern vibe.
  • • Hungarikum Bisztró A local favorite with classic Hungarian dishes done right. Great atmosphere and generous portion sizes.
  • • Parasztkonyha Restaurant Rustic, traditional Hungarian fare with strong reviews and a cozy setting.
  • • Százéves Étterem One of Budapest's historic restaurants, serving old-school Hungarian comfort food in a charming traditional environment.
  • • Gettó Gulyás Very popular Hungarian restaurant known for quality goulash and classic Hungarian plates.
  • • Sir Lancelot – Medieval Restaurant A medieval-themed restaurant where back in the days people ate with their hands. They also have evening shows with music and dance — a fun and unique experience.
  • • Iguana – Mexican Restaurant Best fajitas so far!! A lively spot for margaritas and a fun dinner.
  • • Oriental Soup House Amazing pho and perfect if you're looking for something lighter.

♨️ Thermal Baths

Budapest — and Hungary in general — is world-famous for its thermal baths. And to highlight: these places are clean and well maintained.

Rudas Baths

Our personal favourite. It features a panoramic rooftop pool with breathtaking views of the Danube and the bridges.

  • • Open every day
  • • Night bathing on weekends (separate ticket required)
  • • The historic Turkish bath section is gender-separated on weekdays and mixed on weekends
  • • The wellness pools are mixed every day

https://www.rudasfurdo.hu/en

Széchenyi Thermal Bath

The most famous and iconic thermal bath in Budapest.

It is known for its large outdoor pools surrounded by beautiful yellow Neo-Baroque architecture. In winter, the rising steam creates a magical atmosphere. It's lively, social, and offers the classic Budapest bath experience.

https://www.szechenyibath.hu/

⭐ Discover Esztergom – Réka's Hometown

If you have extra time during your stay, we highly recommend a day trip to Esztergom, one of Hungary's most historic towns, located about 1–1.5 hours from Budapest.

There is also the opportunity to go by Danube cruise, which takes approximately 4–5 hours and offers a scenic journey along the river. Alternatively, there is an easy direct train connection of about one hour.

Esztergom was once the capital of Hungary and remains the spiritual center of the country. Its main highlight is the Esztergom Basilica, the largest church in Hungary and one of the largest in Europe.

The basilica sits high above the Danube and offers breathtaking panoramic views — you can even see Slovakia just across the river.

If you would like to visit the city, please let us know — we would be happy to prepare a separate full-day plan… or even go together 🤍
